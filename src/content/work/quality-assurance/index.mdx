* **Manual Testing:** Perform functional, regression, smoke, and sanity testing to ensure features work as expected.
* **Bug Reporting:** Identify, document, and prioritize bugs with clear reproduction steps, expected vs. actual results, and supporting evidence.
* **Test Case Design:** Create structured test cases covering positive, negative, and edge-case scenarios.
* **Test Documentation:** Develop reusable QA templates for bug reporting and test case tracking to organize testing activities and monitor issue resolution.
* **UI/UX Testing:** Validate interface consistency, layout, responsiveness, and usability across screens.
* **Test Execution & Tracking:** Track test results, bug status, and testing progress to support systematic quality assurance.
* **Developer Collaboration:** Communicate findings with developers and verify fixes through retesting.
